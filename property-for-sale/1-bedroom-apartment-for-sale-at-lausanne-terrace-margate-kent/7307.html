About this apartment

Situated on the top floor of a well-maintained building, with attractive and well-presented communal entrance halls, this beautifully presented one-bedroom apartment offers modern, low-maintenance living in one of Margate's most popular and vibrant locations.

Margate has become increasingly sought-after in recent years, well known for its sandy beach, thriving Old Town, independent shops, galleries, and excellent selection of popular restaurants, cafés and bars. The area offers a fantastic lifestyle, with the seafront, Turner Contemporary, and the town's growing food and drink scene all within easy reach.

The property features high ceilings throughout with bright and airy accommodation, and benefits from large windows to both the front and rear, allowing an abundance of natural light throughout while offering far-reaching views. During the warmer summer months, the windows can be opened at both ends of the apartment, creating a lovely natural breeze and making the property feel bright, airy and comfortable.

The accommodation comprises a spacious open-plan living area with a contemporary fitted kitchen, creating an ideal space for both relaxing and entertaining. There is a generous double bedroom and a stylish modern shower room, all finished to a high standard.

The building comprises four flats and is collectively managed by the owners, with the owners jointly responsible for the management and maintenance of the building. Residents also benefit from access to a secure bike storage to rear.

Further advantages include a share of the freehold, a long lease, and the property is offered to the market with no onward chain and vacant possession, making it an ideal first-time purchase for those looking to get a foot on the property ladder, as well as an excellent buy-to-let investment opportunity or air b n b!
